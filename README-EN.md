#### &raquo; First ISP Experience: SCW Telecom and Its Restructuring

&raquo;&raquo; My first experience with networks beyond the LAN profile (private Class B or C networks with NAT at the exit router and a single public IP) began with a small 512 kbps contracted link with 8 IP addresses for personal use.  

I also configured the network and internet connectivity for the Sanitary and Environmental Engineering and Structural Engineering departments at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG) — each department with a valid and routable /24 block — during my tenure at Comjota. By that point, I already had a clear inclination toward advanced computer networking studies.

A defining milestone in my career was the management of ASN 28138 at SCW Telecom — an ISP holding an IPv4 /20 block and an IPv6 /32 block in the process of implementation. This expanded my experience from LAN environments to CAN and MAN networks, encompassing both Ethernet cabling and fiber optic and wireless point-to-point implementations. I also gained experience with Ubiquiti and Mikrotik systems.

SCW Telecom is an ISP operating ASN 28138 in the city of São Carlos. The provider was without a systems administrator at the time — having lost numerous clients due to downtime and recurring incidents.

I encountered legacy solutions that were no longer viable to maintain. Servers were running Slackware, and some used VirtualBox Headless as a hypervisor — with no documentation or standardization. Peering agreements were documented solely within the border router configurations, requiring reverse engineering to begin reestablishing sessions.

All critical infrastructure was reimplemented using Gentoo Hardened — leveraging Grsec and PAX security patches with RBAC control — and virtualization was deployed with Xen + Flask/XSM on Gentoo Hardened, adhering to the distribution's official hardening guidelines. I created comprehensive documentation and hosted it in an external Mercurial repository. This experience, being my first in Telecommunications, was directly responsible for my passion and introduction to the world of Telecommunications and MetroEthernet networks.

#### &raquo; Development of the zimbrahelpdesk System

&raquo;&raquo; Santa Casa de Misericórdia de Belo Horizonte — a client of Comjota — reported the need for a lightweight, console-based system to allow password resets on the email platform in cases of lockout caused by login errors, while ensuring that Helpdesk staff would not have privileged access to the email system.  

Leveraging the availability of terminal clients across all workstations, I developed the zimbrahelpdesk application using the [2502](https://github.com/caetanus/resume/blob/master/resume-pt_br.rst#2502) project as the communication layer — providing a clean console interface with PAM-based access control for the password reset system — approved and deployed by IT management.
